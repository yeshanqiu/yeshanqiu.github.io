DATA SOURCES & ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Bird occurrence records are drawn from the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF.org) and eBird (Cornell Lab of Ornithology), contributed by citizen scientists worldwide.

Urban form and land-cover metrics draw on OpenStreetMap (road network), the World Settlement Footprint (DLR, building footprints, height and volume), the ETH Global Canopy Height 2020 dataset (canopy cover, height and structure), GLOBMAP surface water, ESRI 10m Annual Land Use / Land Cover, WorldPop (population), and the Global Human Settlement Layer Urban Centre Database (city boundaries).

Full dataset-level citations available on request.
